02The director

A gaze shaped by
place, people & performance.

Nithin Bharadwaj is a filmmaker, director and writer from Puttur, Karnataka, whose creative sensibilities are rooted in the cultural landscape of South India.

Growing up surrounded by music, Yakshagana and the arts, he developed an early fascination for storytelling, performance and the visual language of cinema. That instinct eventually led him away from medical studies and towards the film set.

At the heart of his work is a simple pursuit: to make local voices feel specific, alive and universally understood.
